AimCore v1.0 is out!

The first complete version of AimCore is here. What started as a simple tool for practicing my aim has grown into a full training suite.

  • Full gamepad support, with its own stick sensitivity
  • Sensitivity Finder: calculates your ideal sensitivity for mouse and gamepad
  • Training profiles with timed sessions of 30, 60 or 120 seconds
  • Crosshair presets you can export and share as a text code
  • Remappable controls for gameplay and menus
  • Best results saved per scenario
